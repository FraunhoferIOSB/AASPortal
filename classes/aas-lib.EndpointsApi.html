    Class EndpointsApi

    This service provides methods to interact with the AAS endpoints API, allowing for management of endpoints and retrieval of AAS documents and their content. It includes functionality for adding, updating, and removing endpoints, as well as fetching documents and handling file uploads and downloads related to AAS packages.
